TikCryption
TikCryption is software capable of encrypting and decrypting your messages! I will attempt to make it stronger over each iteration, though it should not be relied on for serious or private matters.



The Nameless Hide Function
I am sure there are a many a people who have had their messages spied on by those around them. The hide function makes it harder for unintended recipients to view messages by creating a privacy-like screen, which stops people from reading the whole message! It also looks really cool when you edit the colours, size and opacity in settings.



Coin Simulator
It literally simulates the model of a coin. It does nothing else.



Cibophobia
The first original web game, Cibophobia follows the journey of a crab as it endures waves of food!

- Coin Simulator -


"Why use a real coin when you can use a completely useless virtual coin instead?"


Coin simulator allows you to do just that! You can now play with a useless coin that serves no function at all! Spin to your heart's content!


Dev Notes:
- You can pause the coin with spacebar.
- You can spin it by left clicking the coin.
- You can edit the faces and background of the coin.

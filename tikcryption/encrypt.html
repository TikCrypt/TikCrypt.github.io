How to Use:
1 - Input text into the textarea below.
2 - Click the encrypt button to encrypt your message.
3 - Copy your encrypted text (and the key) and use the decrypt tool to decrypt the text.



Notes:
- DO NOT trust TikCryption for serious matters.
- Key's persist from the decryption to encryption pages!
- Your message doesn't, so make sure you copy it!
- TikCryption also works in some other languages such as Chinese.
